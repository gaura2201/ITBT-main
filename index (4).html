About the business

What we do

We book rail wagons and rakes for businesses, handle loading, first and last mile trucking, and keep you updated until delivery.

Target customers

Manufacturers, steel and cement plants, farm produce traders, FMCG distributors and exporters moving bulk or container cargo.

Service area

Western India hub in Vadodara with corridors to Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai and the ports of Mundra and Kandla.

Key value

Up to 30% lower cost per tonne-km than road on long hauls, lower emissions, and one dashboard for every consignment.

Services

Full wagon booking

Reserve covered, open or tank wagons for a single customer.

Container rail

20ft and 40ft container movement between terminals and ports.

Door-to-door

Trucking at both ends so cargo leaves your gate and arrives at theirs.

Live tracking

Location, ETA and delivery partner for every order, any time.
